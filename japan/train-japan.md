## Raw Notes

- Top line train tips
    - Hyperdia.com, Japan Travel by NAVITIME, all JR websites
- You can pay with your IC card (Suica, Pasmi, etc), but you need to register online first with a credit card
    - tap on/tap off, can be loaded onto smart phone
- Takanawa Gateway station serves as a test station for various groups of robot assistants
- Frequent usa of English signage and announcements, a detailed map system and the Japanese people's famous attentive service make using the rail system a delight.
- Inner city trains ... require their own special passes to ride.
- JR pass: 7, 14, or 21 days
    - Shinkansen, JR express, limited express lines.
    - Take long journeys, as many as you can, to make the most of it
    - Nozomi and Mizuho trains (faster, more frequent) now available with a hefty supplement
- The Kansai Wide Area Pass: 5 days
- The Kyun pass: 1 day, JR East, Kanto and Tohoku regions.
    - Aomori, Sendai, Kanazawa, Nagano, Niigata, Morioka
- Major cities have tourist passes
- JR West All Area Pass? Kansai Hokuriku Area Pass?
- JR offices at major stations have bright green and white seat logo
    - Machines have JR pass button bottom-left, ask for JR Pass QR code, ask for passport number
    - Can book some online aki-net.com/en/jreast-train-reservation , jreast.co.jp
- **Reserve. Many trains are reservation-only.**
- Unlike other trains, you can eat on the shinkansen

### Kyoto and Kyoto daytrips

- Kyoto is much smaller than Tokyo or Osaka and the train network can be explored easily
- Daytrips to places like Arashiyama, Nara, and Uji are fast to access
- Trains take you to mountain temples
- Central area of Shijo-dori is just a few stops from Kyoto station
- Subway 1-day pass or Kansai Railway pass (2 or 3 days)

- Stay over in verdant Kibune
    - Forests, mountain streams, waterfalls and rocky outcrops
- Temple hopping the Keihan line
    - Gion, Hanamikoji-dori, Kennin-ji temple

### Train Japan Essentials

- N'EX or Keisei Skyliner - all seats reservation only
- Tap tickets, but JR Passes go into the gate slot, don't forget to pick it up at the other side!
- Large bag rules, check online rules: [Oversized Luggage at West JR](https://www.westjr.co.jp/travel-information/en/train-usage-guide/luggage/)
    - Bags under 160cm can be stored on overhead racks
    - Bags bigger will be charged an "oversized" bag fee, and need to reserve in advance
    - Well-priced delivery services for bag, e.g. Yamato (cat carrying kitten logo)
- Not all ATMS take international cards, try a Seven Bank or large stores, post offices
- Many small towns are still cash-based
