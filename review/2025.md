- A Prayer for the Crown-shy by Chambers, Becky. Science Fiction.
- A Psalm for the Wild-Built by Chambers, Becky. Science Fiction.
- The Mimicking of Known Successes by Older, Malka. Science Fiction.
- Living Beautifully with Uncertainty and Change by Chödrön, Pema. Zen

- The Empusium: A Health Resort Horror Story by Tokarczuk, Olga. Horror.
