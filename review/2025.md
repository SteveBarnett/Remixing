## In [books](/reading/2025/)

I read almost a hundred books this year. That seems like a lot! About 8 books a month. A few of the highlights:

- Private Rites by Armfield, Julia. Fiction
- Rose/House by Martine, Arkady. Science Fiction
- How to Raise an Ox (Shobogenzo) by Cook, Francis Dojun. Zen.
- A Prayer for the Crown-shy by Chambers, Becky. Science Fiction.
- A Psalm for the Wild-Built by Chambers, Becky. Science Fiction.
- The Mimicking of Known Successes by Older, Malka. Science Fiction.
- Living Beautifully with Uncertainty and Change by Chödrön, Pema. Zen
- Mindful Self-Compassion for Burnout by Neff, Kristin. Psychology.
- The Empusium: A Health Resort Horror Story by Tokarczuk, Olga. Horror.
