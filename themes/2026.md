These are very similar to last year. One notable change is Work to Vocation to more accurately reflect my approach to it.

## Valued Action: RICC

Continued recommitment to aligning my actions with my values.

- **R**efrained, **I**mperfect, **C**elebratory, **C**aring
- Or: **R**efrainer, **I**mperfectionist, **C**elebrator, **C**arer

These aren't the only things that are important. They are the ones I want to focus on.

Other ways to approach valued action: as koans; as precepts.
