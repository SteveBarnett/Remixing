I've been coming back to this idea of **turning flow into stock** . Here I've applied it to my current "gates" to see if it brings up anything interesting or potentially fruitful.

A general theme is the building blocks kind of idea: each bit of stock, or level of stock, can serve as a foundation or a base for the next bit(s) of stock.

## Vocation

- The most obvious one is writing on the work blog. But it can also be talks and workshops. They will roll out a bit more naturally.
- Idea: regularly write up what I worked on. Once a month sounds good. PD Day sounds like the right time to do it.

## Health

- In particular stacking things. Getting a base layer going, then adding on top of it. This is partly about small increases to make them easy to add.
- There's also a flow problem here. I keep getting knocked out of it.
- [ ] How to make flow more consistent? How to not get knocked off? How to get back to it quickly?

## Interests

- Project-based work. Having a container for a set of creative ideas and outputs. But... I already know this. So the problem here is the flow: keeping it going, restarting it.
- [ ] How to wave that flow in?

## Practice

- There's daily sitting. That acts as a stock into flow. I do feel the cumulative effects of an ongoing zazen practice.
- Then there's the writing. I do this a bunch, and do irregular gathering of topics. I think a broader, more regular, gathering might be helpful. Quarterly was my first instinct. But maybe monthly would be better. It doesn't matter if it's multiple topics. The goal is to slosh around the ideas and see the topic if there is one. Doing this with end-of-month reflection seems like it could work well.
