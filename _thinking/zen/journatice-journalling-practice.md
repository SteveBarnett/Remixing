A practice with some similarities to [Philotation](/thinking/philosophical-meditation/).

## Overview

Orient to the truth of direct experience, beneath the conceptual knowing

- By observing and identifying our history and habitual patterns, we lessen and soften the patterns and beliefs.
    - By releasing pent-up emotions, we release the energy being employed to repress and suppress them. 
    - Internally feeling the fullness of it, not outwardly expressing it or showing it to someone
- Our sense of self is altered by our changed relationship to these habitual patterns.

## Journalling

History → Current → Helpful 

- What is your lifetime history and experience with this emotion?
- In what circumstances does it arise?
- How is it helpful to you in your present life?

## Practice

Connecting directly to the pain and noticing it's not the whole body often makes it more manageable.

Where → What else → Allow

- Where exactly in your body you feel the somatic experience?
- What else is there, in your body?
- Allow both/all.
