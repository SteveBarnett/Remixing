- When one understands that the distinction between life and death is as arbitrary and as illusory as all other distinctions, one finds the solution in the disappearance of the problem.

## Poems

> A tune of non-being<br>Filling the void:<br>Spring sun<br>Snow whiteness<br>Bright clouds<br>Clear wind.

> My whole life long I've sharpened my sword<br>And now, face to face with death<br>I unsheath it, and lo–<br>The blade is broken–<br>Alas!

> Empty-handed I entered the world<br>Barefoot I leave it.<br>My coming, my going–<br>Two simple happenings<br>That got entangled.

> It lights up<br>as lightly as it fades:<br>a firefly.

> All moving things<br>come to an end:<br>a knotty willow.

> My old body:<br>a drop of dew grown<br>heavy at the leaf tip.

> Moon in a barrel:<br>you never know just when<br>the bottom will fall out.

> Ice in a hot world:<br>my life<br>melts.

> I shift my pillow<br>closer to the<br>full moon.

> I wake and find<br>the coloured iris<br>I saw in my dream.

> Spitting blood<br>clears up reality<br>and dream alike.
