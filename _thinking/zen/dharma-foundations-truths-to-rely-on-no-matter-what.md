Notes from [Dharma Foundations: Truths to Rely on No Matter What](https://zenstudiespodcast.com/dharma-foundation/)

- Dharma Foundation 1: **In Reality, we are boundaryless and interdependent**
    - You are empty of any inherently existing, independent, autonomous, enduring self-nature 
    - It is an illusion that your life is run by an internal “Executive I” who calls all the shots. In reality, your life is an unfolding of causes and conditions within which you have precious moments of choice.
- Dharma Foundation 2: There is immense and transformative power in actions that transcend the apparent boundary between self and other
    - Such actions of body, speech, and mind include goodwill, kindness, compassion, sympathetic joy, generosity, and beneficial action.
- Dharma Foundation 3: The flow of generosity transcends the intentions or relative resources of individuals
    - In Buddhism we call this “the emptiness of the three spheres of giver, receiver, and gift.”
- Dharma Foundation 4: All beings have Buddha-Nature, in fact all Being is Buddha-Nature
    - Just because we can’t perceive it at any given moment doesn’t mean it isn’t true. 
- Dharma Foundation 5: What matters most is intangible; although we naturally fear loss, suffering, and chaos, what matters most can’t be taken from us
    - The fundamental teaching of Buddhism is that our experience of life is mostly dependent on the state of our own mind and heart. Regardless of our circumstances, there are choices we can make to give us strength and peace of mind.
    - If we are living fortunate lives, it’s very difficult not to become reliant on conditional sources of happiness.
- Dharma Foundation 6: **The way to freedom lies in facing the truth**, no matter how difficult it is to accept, and with practice even difficult things need not overwhelm us
    - It seems counterintuitive, but in reality, the way to freedom – even joy – lies in facing the truth. It doesn’t matter what that truth is. 
- Dharma Foundation 7: No matter what happens, we can practice and take refuge in Buddha, Dharma, and Sangha
- Dharma Foundation 8: In addition to the dependent dimension of Reality – the dimension of time, space, and causality that we are usually aware of – it has an independent dimension in which all things are precious and luminous in their own suchness
