## Gathered notes

- Unexpressed emotions often turn inward
- Seek to learn, understand more deeply
- Do nice things more other people
- It's the situation that upsets us, but our resistance to it
- Don't let the rope of conversation go slack for very long
- Hang around people who bring out the best in you
- Rather than overthinking a problem, rest and relax, and let the solution arise
- To become an expert, learn from your many mistakes
- Be comfortable with the parts of yourself you feel uncomfortable about
- Rather than haggling with your past, let it be, so it can flow like a river
- The people we dislike teach us a lot about our mind
- "Let it go" means "Accept it fully"
- The struggles of life are what improve us

## Raw notes

### Chapter One - Self-care

- When we become kinder to ourselves, we can become kinder to the world
- ... since you do not know how to properly express your own feelings, the anger that ought to be directed toward its instigator is trapped inside you and ends up attacking you instead.
- When you care for yourself first, the world will also find you worthy of care
- Life naturally takes a toll on our bodies, our minds, and our relationships. ... choose a life that improves through failure and pain.
- Don't think you are lovable only when you succeed at what the world demands of you
- ... one of the know causes of becoming a workaholic is growing up feeling unworthy of your parents attention unless you do something great
- If you feel your self-esteem is low, try doing something nice for a stranger.

### Chapter Three - Empathy

- May my suffering become the seed of compassion
- Rather than trying to improve someone, just be a mirror, reflecting them without judgement
- "Your perspective reveals something that I had been unaware of. Tell me more, because I want to understand it."

### Chapter Four - Relationships

- What distresses us is less the circumstances we find ourselves in and more the energy we expend in resisting them
- When we do not try and posses someone and just enjoy their company, the connection endures
- Do not let the rope of conversation go slack for very long
- If you listen to someone tell a story about someone else, in many cases more is revealed about the speaker than the one they are speaking about
- Cultivate a deeper connection with those people in hose company you like yourself best

### Chapter Five - Courage

- We have become accustomed to sacrificing the present for the sake of the future
- Excessive thought won't solve a problem. Rather than trying to fix it by thinking, set your mind at ease. A solution will rise to the surface. Remember that wisdom comes from stillness.
- Do not be afraid of making mistakes. Be afraid only of not learning from your mistakes. An expert is someone who has acquired skills and knowledge by making a lot of mistakes.
- The first step to overcoming failure is to fully admit you have failed
- If you try to conceal the inferior part of yourself while feeling ashamed of it, it will continue to be a problem. No-one really cares about it if you are comfortable with it.

### Chapter Six - Healing

- Rather than fight our feelings, we should honour them by allowing them to be there and witnessing how their energy moves inside us
- Observe your emotions attentively and compassionately
- We are quick to believe that out thoughts are a clear reflection of reality when this is absolutely no the case
- Thoughts are only fleeting responses to specific circumstances
- When you care about the problems of others, you realise that yours are neither big nor unique

### Chapter Seven - Enlightenment

- ... the mind stays in the present moment because the place of enlightenment that practitioners is in the here and now
- Formless and immaculate silence is your true nature
- Any object that can be observed is not your true nature
- Rather than haggling with your past, let it be, so it can flow like a river
- There is no better teacher than the people you dislike as they get you to examine your mind more deeply
- According to the Heart Sutra, you are liberated when you realise there is nothing to attain

### Chapter Eight - Acceptance

- "Let it go" is actually another way of expressing "Accept it fully." It does not mean that the painful memory will disappear.
- You are not the wound, but the deep silence that knows the wound
- Love's true face is acceptance and freedom, not restriction and control
- Labelling something a problem is often what makes it one
- If life were free of adversity, we wouldn't have any opportunities to grow. It's in our struggling to solve the challenges that life throws at us that our talents are honed and our endurance builds.
