## Raw notes

### The Naropa University 2014 Commencement Address

- We all want to succeed, especially if you consider success as "**it works out the way I want it to**."
- Welcome the unwelcome
- We move away from the rawness, of holding the vulnerability in our heart, when we blame things on other people.
- One of the ways to help yourself hold failure is to **question what is really happening when there is a failure.**
- **Can you allow yourself to feel what you feel when things don't go the way you want them to?** When things don't go the way you hoped and wished for and longed for them to go?
- **Instead of the habitual** labelling of "failure" or "loser" or **thinking there is something wrong with you**, you could **get curious about what is going on**.
- "Maybe what is happening here is not that I am a failure–I am just hurting."
- It's in that space–where we aren't masking ourselves or trying to make circumstances go away–that our best qualities begin to shine.

### Leaning In To The Sharp Points – Conversation with Tami Simon

- At a very primitive level, we associate **discomfort**, which can come from feeling like we've failed, with danger, so even if the discomfort is subtle–a small discomfort–at some level **our brain process it as danger, as something to get rid of**.
- **This view is about turning toward instead of turning away.**
- if [what you're saying to yourself] is very self-critical, if it's very harsh, don't believe what's being said. Or you can just rephrase the self-critical talk so it's more gentle and positive.
- Let the regret pierce you to the heart, and then you can lay it aside so that you don't have to carry it with you for the rest of your life as a package.
- ... to be human is it have all these different qualities.
- **"You don't try to get rid of these feelings, you come to know them, and you can hold them in your heart as part of your being a human being."**
- **Allowing yourself to get dragged down builds up this huge sense of "me".**
- Failing better means when these things happen in your life, they become a source of growth, a source of forward.
