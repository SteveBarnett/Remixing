Draft of a kind of a [Philotation](/thinking/philosophical-meditation/) / [Journatice](/thinking/zen/journatice-journalling-practice/#journalling) mashup.

- Note → Unpack → Reassure/plan
    - Unpack: History → Current → Helpful

## Note (What?)

- What’s it really about?

## Unpack (So what?)

- When else has this arisen historically?
- How is it helpful to you in your present life?
- How would you the best version of you act?

## Reassure (Now what?)

- How would you be okay, in the worst case?
- What’s helped in the past?
- How would you advise a friend?
