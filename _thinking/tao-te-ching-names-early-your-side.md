- Loosen your grip on **naming things** and thoughts.
- Take small, careful, **early action**.
- Keep up **your side** of things.

## Snips

- Too much naming and thinking creates wanting and chasing. Wanting and chasing drives you crazy.
- Start early. Stay calm and clear. Be careful and caring.
- Keep your side of the contract. Don't worry about theirs.

## Grabs

- Do not-doing
- To be good for everything, don't compete
- Chasing drives people crazy
- To go on and on, don't exist for yourself, don't require fulfilment
- Naming things too much causes danger
- In not naming is not wanting, in not wanting is stillness
- To be comfortable in the world, stay calm and clear
- Do things early to do things easily
- Prefer brave caution to brave daring
- Don’t make demands on others, fulfil your own obligations

## Quotes

### 7 Dim Brightness

### 8 Easy by nature

> True goodness<br>is like water.<br>Water's good<br>for everything.<br>It doesn't compete.

### 12 Not wanting

> Racing, chasing, hunting<br>drives people crazy.

### 15 People of power

> To follow the Way<br>is not to need fulfilment.<br>Unfulfilled, one may live on<br>needing no renewal.

### 37 Over all

### 45 Real power

> To be comfortable in the cold, keep moving;<br>to be comfortable in the heat, hold still;<br>to be comfortable in the world, stay calm and clear.
