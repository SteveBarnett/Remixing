Notes from the [Pace Layers 02025 Annual Journal](https://longnow.org/shop/book-pacelayers-02025/)

## Fashion

- Culture cut free to experiment creatively and irresponsibly, driving energy for commerce
- McLuhan: "The content on any medium is always another medium"
- Ken Wilber's pre/trans fallacy
    - the idiot `<->` genius meme
    - pre-rational intuition is equated with transcendent post-rational insights
- **AI slop is made to please the largest possible audience, yet satisfies no-one**
    - It prioritises economic function
    - The centre of the bell curve
- **Every generation outs technology, and the monetary gains it brings, above creativity**
    - Creativity creates a technology, everyone focuses on replicating the technology, but not on supporting the underlying creativity

## Commerce

- The proper role commerce is to both exploit and absorb the shocks of rapid change
- doughnut economics . org
    - thriving in balance, not endless growth
- (look further ahead, longer now)

## Infrastructure

- Education and science are intellectual infrastructure
    - High yield, but delayed payback
- "You should consider the essential art of civilisation is maintenance" – Pete Seeger
- The necessity of maintenance accumulates invisibly and gradually
- Mission command: say the what, leave autonomy on the how
- **Sustainment: the capacity of a system to endure** (US military term)
- (Book: System Sustainment - Peter Sandborn, William Lucyshyn. Unrelenting endurance, iterative)
- (Is digital preservation the best idea? That times out too. What about how Japanese temples are rebuilt: using the old ways)

## Governance

- Reins in commerce and underwrites infrastructure, **absorbing shocks from above, respecting constraints from below** (culture, nature)
- We should yoke technology to a social purpose
