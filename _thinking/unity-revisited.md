- Everything comes from Reality's infinite causes and conditions
- Everything shows up together all at once

    - Like the unity of birds and sky, fish and water
- Embodiment without adding the story of the self
- The ongoing and complete flux and flow
- The process, the path
- The deep interconnectedness and interwovenness of everything
