Parry. Like avoid, bypass, elude, evade, rebuff.

Like [yield](/thinking/yield-word-salad/). Prevailing by transforming the relationship, by using softness, connection, sensitivity and calmness.

See also: riposte. Like retort. Or counterattack. Like reprisal, reciprocation.
