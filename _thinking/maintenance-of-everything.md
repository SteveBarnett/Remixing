## Raw notes

### Introduction

- **The necessity of maintenance accumulates invisibly and gradually.**
- **When you take responsibility for something, you enter into a contract to take care of it.**

### Chapter One: The Maintenance Race

- **Skilled maintainers advise never trying to solve a new or complex problem without a thorough mulling first.**
- Old systems break in familiar ways. New systems break in unexpected ways.
- Obsession with detail is a hallmark of the most successful maintainers.

### Chapter Two: Vehicles (and weapons)

- The upkeep of any machine is largely about tending to the four sources of most problems: moving parts, flowing fluids, flowing electricity, and temperature stresses.
- **Beware iatrogenic repair–when a sloppy attempt to fix a problem makes the problem worse or adds a new one.**
- To push through the frustration takes a level of zeal that he calls "gumption.""
- Of the seven gumption traps he names Pirsig  says five lay in the mechanic; he calls them "hang-ups". The other four in the machine; he calls these "setbacks".
- **Another element of mastery, Pirsig says, is to acquire the best tools you can afford and become skilled with them.**
- Ford built the car to be so robust that you could drive it anywhere, and so simple and transformable that you were invited to adapt it to your own needs.
- He designed a car so cheap that anyone could buy it and so uncomplicated that anyone could learn to do the maintenance it required. Once customers took that on, they discovered that **the power to maintain is the power to improve.**
- [Snap go/no-go gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Go/no-go_gauge#Snap_gauge)
- Low maintenance sells. This is a key driver of progress in the world.
- **There's no reward when you do it and no reward later–just the unnoticeable absence of pain.**
- RTFM!
- **Old systems fail in familiar and prepared-for ways. New systems fail in new and unprepared-for ways.**
- **The route to simplicity  was continuous paring down. Repeated field testing** ... led to hundreds of refinements, each enabling greater reliability and ease of use.
- Cheap, clunky, sturdy, and maintainable wins time after time.
- **Maintainence prowess is core to rapid adaptivity under duress.**
- **(Mission command style improves operational tempo)**
- Cross-training builds mutual respect  and sharp teamwork, and it makes training lively when team members' roles are suddenly swapped.
