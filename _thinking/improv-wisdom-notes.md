## Intro

- Do something useful, give something back
- Human interdependence
- The only real failure is not doing anything

## Maxims

1. Say yes
    - Accept, don't try and control
2. Don't prepare
    - Be ready to receive unexpected gifts
    - Rarely are you being judged
3. Just show up
    - Take a step towards something
    - Try new locations
4. Start anywhere
    - You'll gain perspective as you work
5. Be average
6. Pay attention
    - Look outside yourself
    - Notice new things in old places
7. Face the facts
    - Insecurity, flux, is the norm
8. Stay the course
    - "What is my purpose now?"
9. Wake up to the gifts
10. Make mistakes, please
    - Mistakes are inevitable
    - It's what you do after that counts
    - Resilience, perseverance
11. Act now
    - Do what is needed
12. Take care of each other
    - Make your partner look good
13. Enjoy the ride
    - Enjoy what you are doing, don't wait for an "enjoyable thing"
