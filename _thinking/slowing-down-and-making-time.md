- There's no need to be busy.

- When you slow down, you have more time.

## [Practice the Art of Slowing Down - Tricycle: The Buddhist Review](https://tricycle.org/article/slowing-down-buddhism/)

- A daily practice for realizing the counterintuitive truth that when you slow down, you have more time.
- I practiced not letting my mind leave my body—not getting ahead of myself. That one mindfulness exercise changed how I went through the day.
- Rushing reinforces the sense of time pressure. You feel squeezed, busy, harassed by time. Slowing down conduces to ease, gentleness, and relaxation.
- Feel for the inner imperative that makes you feel busy. That compresses your sense of self into a forward-pushing agent called me. Focused on what I’m doing and where I am going. What happens if you soften and slow, just a little bit? Feel how that changes your experience. Your sense of yourself. Your capacity for ease in the moment.

## [Making Time for Meditation When Busy by Ayya Khema - Tricycle](https://tricycle.org/magazine/meditation-when-busy/)

- There’s No Need to Be Busy
- ... not get caught up in too much bustle. How many committees do I belong to? How busy am I? Do I think I have no time for meditation because I’m so busy?
- When we think we have no time for meditation, we should immediately consider whether we have time for eating.
- for that which is important, we always have time.
- Being caught up in too much bustle brings with it a distracted mind.
- to be overly busy cannot possibly bring peacefulness.
- The more we try to prove that we are somebody, the less we have a chance to become nobody. And that’s what nirvana is all about. It doesn’t sound appealing to some people, because they haven’t had enough dukkha yet. When we’ve had enough dukkha with the somebody, we can actually appreciate the fact that there’s only one way to get out of dukkha, and that’s being nobody.
- Unlocking it takes time, and it takes the quiet mind, the contented mind, the satisfied mind. It needs the mind which knows that there is something to be found far beyond anything at all that we can ever find in the world
