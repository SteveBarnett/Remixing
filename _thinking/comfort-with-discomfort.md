When am I comfortable(ish) when I would expect not to be? When am I comfortable with discomfort?

Thinking about using the Heath brothers Bright Spots as a way into Practice.

## To Do (ish)

- Remember POCK, especially Patient, Open, Clear
- Do more things that make you (slightly!) uncomfortable

## Summary

- Something that seems to be popping up is basically **habituation**. Getting used to something over time lessens the discomfort. Gaining experience in it.
    - A sub-item is **confidence**.
- Another thing is **direction of attention**, and **sense of self**. When I am deeply focused, I'm not worrying, not feeling discomfort. I'm not thinking about myself, what people think of me.
- Another is **don't-know mind**. Being curious and wanting to see what happens, what's going on.
    - Related: **no specific goal** in mind.

Something to do: work with it, [polopposits](/thinking/polopposits/)-style.

## When

- Dating. When I was on the apps and Tindering, I felt a lot less nervous and uncomfortable that I thought I would.
- Public speaking. On paper it sounds like my worst nightmare, but in practice I really enjoy it.
- Hosting. Again, it sounds like a recipe for brain-breaking social anxiety, but I get a lot out of it.
- Solo dining. It used to really bother me. Now it doesn't, hardly at all.
- Ends of relationships. I've been getting better at taking the balanced view, rather than assuming it's all my fault. That includes accepting the things that were my fault, with some grace.
- Trying new recipes. It sometimes doesn't work out well! But that seems fine.
- Acceptance of the discomfort. This so often, so quickly, dissolves it for me. I think that's part of why I use my [three uhds](/thinking/the-three-uhds/) a lot.

The public speaking has an interesting pattern. About ten minutes before, I become intensely nervous. for about the first ten minutes, I stay intensely nervous. After that, I settle into the rhythm, and I realise I do kinda now what I'm doing.

### When more widely

- I have a job to do, something to keep the monkey mind temporarily occupied. Just long enough for me to experience that actually everything is okay.
- I'm not goal-focused, only-one-outcome-is-okay-focused, sitting with don't-know mind.
- I'm mentally engaged. This needs unpacking because it's actually quite easy to knock me out of flow, the zone.
- I'm paying rapt attention to a person or people. When the attention is outward.
- I manage to turn towards the trouble and work it rather than try and avoid it.
- I focus on my values, especially openness, rather than worrying and thinking.
- I'm looking for what's working rather than what's not.
- I take a why-can't-it-be-both stance.
- I'm more interested in something else than being comfortable.
- A Special Interest is triggered.
- When I've had a fair amount of practice.
