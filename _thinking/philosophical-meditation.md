Note → Unpack → Reassure/plan

## Anxiety

- **What's it really about?**
- How would Best You deal with it?
- What's happened in the past? What helped?

**How would you be okay, in the worst case?**

## Upset

- **What did they fail to realise or notice?**
- What scared you?
- What good part of you feels in danger?
- What incident in the past does this remind you of?

**How would you advise a friend?**

## Curiosity/Excitement

- **What call to growth is here in a garbled form?**
- When else hav eyou felt something similar?

**What changes might you make?**
