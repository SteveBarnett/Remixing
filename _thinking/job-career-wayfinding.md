## Raw notes

### A Job To Love

- Know from the inside: ourselves, our jobs
- Know only from the outsidE: other people, other jobs
    - Everyone is a bit mad
    - Every job has downsides
- Parents: implicit expectations, pass on their dreams and burden
    - If we're doing better than them we're on the right track
- Success: conditional, love, implied verdict on others' choices
    - Might lead us to self-sabotage
- It's normal to be confused: our minds don't like direct questioning
- Look at the qualities of, input to jobs
    - Not their outputs
    - What matches our talents, pleasures?
    - What matches the needs of the world?

## Creative Class

- Define why you do the work you do: use it as a powerful decision-making filter
- Passion often follows mastery
- Create a future-facing portfolio
