## Disconnect from the world to connect with yourself

You must play a little hide-and-seek in order to produce something worth being found.

Silence and solitude are crucial.

A bliss station can be not just a *where*, but also a *when*. ... one can make up for a lack of the other.

## You can be woke without waking to the news

Many artists have discovered that they work best upon waking, when their mind is fresh, and they're still in a quasi-dream state.

## Airplane mode can be a way of life

> "The phone gives us a lot but it takes away three keys elements of discovery: loneliness, uncertainty, and boredom. Those have always been where creative ideas come from." – Lynda Barry

## Learn how to say no

> "I must decline, for secret reasons." – E. B. White
