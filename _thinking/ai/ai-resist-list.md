## Raw notes

These systems consume an unfathomable amount of data, land, energy, labor, and water. They are rooted in profoundly disturbing ideologies that seek to flatten the world into a “one size fits all” abstraction and to replace humans with machines.

We call the companies leading this form of AI development “empires”. Under the guise of a civilizing mission to "benefit all of humanity”, they use large-scale AI development as cover to consolidate resources, destroy ecosystems, centralize information, hollow out institutions, and gain paramount economic and political power.

It doesn’t have to be this way. As AI researchers, journalists, and critical scholars, we have built, documented, and imagined radical alternatives that do precisely the opposite: center community, celebrate human agency, honor local context and history, and rejuvenate the planet.

---

Nothing about the current trajectory of AI development is inevitable. It was shaped by the thousands of subjective decisions of a tiny elite, and continues its march based on the active participation and tacit consent of people globally.

### Call out the Hype

The empires run on mythmaking and hype about what AI is, what it can do, and what they need to build it. Deflating that hype and countering myth with reality diminishes the empire's influence.

- Change the language
- Decenre US tech giants
- Document when AI fails

### Follow the Funders

The empires cannot function without massive injections of capital. Holding funders accountable can weaken the financial footing of the AI industry and stall their advancement.

### Take Back our Data

Large-scale AI models require large-scale data sets to train on. Without such vast quantities of data, these systems would not be able to exist in their current form.

Document user privacy infringements

### Fight Extractive Data Centers

Similarly, the empires seek to secure an unparalleled amount of computing power in the form of data centers and supercomputers to train and deploy their systems. Without such scales of computing power, companies would be forced to find more sustainable, less extractive methods for AI development.

### Combat Resource Extraction

Data centers are made from rare earth minerals and other resources. Evidence shows that bottlenecks in these resources have already forced the AI industry to slow its pace.

### Advocate for the Rights of Workers

The empires require the labor of workers across their supply chain, whether to mine minerals, construct data centers, annotate data, train models, or adopt their products in other industries. Collective worker action can stall the AI industry’s reckless growth and hold it accountable to higher standards.

- Form or join a labour coalition
- Bring transparency on the use of AI in the workplace
- Unionise and organise

### Just Say No

The AI industry needs a broad user base to develop a viable business model and public legitimacy. Weak or declining user numbers would force companies to change tack.

- Create resistance tools
- Challenge the necessity of AI adoption
- Reduce your dependence on Big Tech
- Refuse the use of GenAI in education

### Challenge Permissive Governance

Governments around the world strengthen the empires with hugely permissive, and often enabling, legal and regulatory environments. Unraveling these policy structures and instituting real accountability mechanisms can help contain the AI industry's impunity.

### Reject Surveillance

The empires use various forms of surveillance to control workers, undermine collective action, and suppress dissent. Rejecting this expansive monitoring will help preserve an ecosystem of resistance.
