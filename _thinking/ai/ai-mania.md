## Whittled notes

1. **A piece of work needs to get done**
    - There's pressure to put AI into it somewhere, somehow
2. **It gets AI-washed to get approved**
    - People are incentivised to lie about how much AI is in a project, or if it even needs AI at all
        - If someone says it doesn't need AI, or they need more staff instead of AI, they must be using AI wrong
    - People are incentivised to believe or claim AI-higher-productivity to avoid embarrassment and to keep their job
        - Each link in the chain from client's boss to client to vendor to vendor's boss, in both directions, has to affirm the claim or lose the business, the relationship
        - When you can press for details, "AI is changing everything" becomes "AI *will* change everything"
3. **The project serves as proof of AI-higher-productivity, at a new baseline/benchmark**
4. Back to step 1.
