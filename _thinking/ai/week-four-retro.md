This week I was only on the project for three days, so I don't have much to report.

## Bit of a summary

- My favourite bit was when I got to legitimately write about the limitations our of test suite. Something like being honest about it is acting with integrity.
- The verbosity problem is compounding pretty quickly as we proceed.

## What

- Finishing up cards eval: trouble with clickable card vs card-as-link
- Refactoring
- Reviewing PRs
- Carefully reviewing test for accuracy
- Writing website copy
    - Testing Methodology
    - Overview
- Started WCAG SC coverage page

## What went well

- The writing was fun.
- Starting to get the hang of reviewing PRs. The approach we took was to split up the review, even though the whole PR gets approved. We left reviewing of the accuracy of the test for later, and just reviewed the other parts.
- Claude Code doing the starter summary stats for our WCAG SC coverage.

## What went badly

- I started the writing with a Claude version, but I'm not sure it helped much. I deleted lots and lots of it. And rewrote almost all of the rest to be more in our voice, and more understandable.
- The cards eval was a bit of a slog. I think this was at least partly my fault for trying to do it too quickly.
- Reviewing PRs is still a slog. The problem of reviewing a big PR is made much more with AI because the amount to review is massively increased.
- It's very verbose, even with tweaks. It particular, adding overly big docblocks, or adding change comments. This can be mitigated with more careful reviews, of course. But the plausible-at-a-glance problem makes it tricky.
