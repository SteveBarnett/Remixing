Notes from [Has AI Gone Rogue?](https://calnewport.com/has-ai-gone-rogue/).

> AI systems that operate by autonomously executing LLM-generated plans are a really bad idea.

> LLM-powered Ask → Act → Report agents are not synonymous with AI

Cal Newport bringing some cool-headed sense to things, as usual. Here's my little summary.

* There's been a rush of recent reports claiming AI has gone rogue, based on cybersecurity incidents.
* That isn't quite what happened. It was hacking tools work on a loop of Ask (the LLM)  → Act (the harness) → Report (back to the LLM).
* The LLM generates a plausible but unpredictable plan, because that's how LLMS work.
* The incidents happened when the hacking system with access to powerful tools followed that plan, operating autonomously, without human oversight.
* It was less like "AI gone rogue!", and more like "Hacking tool acting on plausible but unpredictable plan does unpredictable things!"

This kind of reporting makes sense when you look at the incentives. Keeping people scared is good for business, feeding into the hype. Presenting LLMs as the key to breakthroughs is good if you're an LLM provider.
