## Web Directions "AI Engineer World's Fair" videos notes


### ["The biggest challenge in your stack? Evals, Evals, Evals" - 2026 State of AI Engineering results](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RGe6EjucbzI)

- (SB: Only) 1,048 respondents
- Newest Engineers are already AI-native
- Audio has highest intent-to-adopt rate (SB: intent is not necessarily a strong signal)
- Accuracy and output quality, agentic capabilities, cost were top three qualities in choosing a model
- **Most teams are using more than one model, usually routing by task type**
- Teams are standardising on platform and tools
- **Cost is now a first-class engineering constraint**
    - Many people adjust their usage based on cost
    - A top-level monitoring concern
- Most allow write access with human-in-the-loop (big increase from last year)
- Agents are governed by many, varied, blunt instrument
- The biggest challenge: evals
    - **Everyone says there are problems in the stack, but everyone has different problems**
- **People buy inference, but product logic, prompts are built in-house**
- Erosion of skills, higher review burden
- Codebase is more brittle
- Sold on the value, worried about the liabilities
