Notes from [Nik Suresh/Ludicity on surviving corporate AI psychosis](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6GOGGzTp3MA)

- Nik's on a lot of sales calls, so he has a wide data set
- "true believers" have gone beyond the trivial concerns of producing working code
- the other group is reasonable people who don't act reasonably
- **AI works as long as it has many good examples to draw from**
- **You're held hostage by the most ridiculous numbers someone has said recently**
- **Most of the AI projects aren't AI projects: it has a small bit of AI, but they brand it an AI project**
- Nik's been fixing failing AI projects
- **Failed AI project cleanup squads will be a thing**
- **Claude is sycophantic, so it makes you think you're clever**
- **your day-today quickly becomes people pasting paragraphs at you**
- And AI will always keep justifying things post-hoc
- Answer Claude with Claude and polish up your resume
- There's ideal moral choices, and then there's feeding your family
- Even good companies are one CEO change away from AI psychosis
- **They pick a task that can be done without an LLM, then pay a consultant to do it with AI**
    - **Asking why upsets people**
- Businesses are famously hostile to hiring people
- Companies are bad at IT projects, this is very well documented
- **Executives tend to have really bad tech advisory**
    - **About the risk profile in particular**
- **SaaS: sycophant as a service**
- **We need sycophancy protections** to stop things hacking our brains
    - **The bots are tuned to maximise time on chat**
- **Don't argue with these people, they're actually nuts**
    - **Just wait for the money to run out**
