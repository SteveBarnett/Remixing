Notes from [How AI Poisons Creativity](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WeCSzEtZcUw&t=1s&pp=ugUEEgJlbg%3D%3D)

## Gathered notes

- AI frames the process as an obstacle.
- But the process is where we learn, where we decide.
- It's the hard part, where we're challenged, where we ask for help.
- Using AI to fix things means we don't learn, and don't want to learn, to avoid making those mistakes in the first place
- Our skills atrophy

## Raw notes

What are the downsides of introducing AI into your workflow?

The AI companies frame the (creative) process as an obstacle.
But actually, you make important decisions along the way.

The hard part, the part that challenges you, the part where you have to ask someone for help. They are the most important part.

AI lets you shortcut and bypass the work of working and learning.
Things that might not be useful right in this moment, but may be later.
The things you let AI fix reduce your desire to learn how to avoid those mistakes / problems in the first place.

You'll lose the ability to even prompt the chatbot well.

Using AI is a classic Faustian bargain.

Strength means resisting the quick and easy solutions.
