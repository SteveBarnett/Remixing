Notes from [Weirdness, the Dao, and Whether Trees have Experiences with philosopher Eric Schwitzgebel on Love & Philosophy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X50PhRiuMjQ).

- The Dao is everything, the processes and patterns, not a collection of stuff and things
- To read: Zhuangzi, "the inner chapters"
- Death, disability, and diversity
- Part of harmonising is celebrating and including differences
- My role is harmonising with what's already here
- There's no formula for answering "what's right?", but there is a mood, a rhythm
- Remember that we use language to discuss things (like consciousness), which is a human invention, inherent;y human-biased
