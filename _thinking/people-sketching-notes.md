## Gathered notes

- Few, partial, expressive lines
- Top to bottom
- Big tapered blocks, then detail
- Head is 1, torso is 3, legs is 3
    - Elbows are 1½, knees, are 1½
    - Dots for shoulders, ankles
- Proportions
    - Ankles to ground is ⅔ head
    - Foot is 1 head long
- Face proportions
    - Brow is ⅓ down from top of head
        - Eyes are ⅓ down from eyebrow to nose
    - Nose bottom is ⅓ down from brow
        - Mouth is ⅓ down from nose to chin
    - Chin is ⅓ down from nose bottom

## Raw notes

### Drawn on the way

#### Drawing figures

Posture reveals emotion.

1. head - outline of hair, face
2. shoulders - angle, distances, relationship to head
3. torso, arms, legs - blocks, exterior contours, points of contact or weight
4. refine figure - emphasise movement, pose, twists

#### Drawing expressive figures

- Be efficient - few, expressive, lines
- Get weird - wonky
- Get loose - emotion, gestures, suggestion
- Be bold - big, swoopy, hand gestures

#### Drawing portraits

When drawing faces, don't think about drawing a face!

1. Forehead and brows
2. Nose
3. Eyes
    - Eyelid fold, lash line
    - Slightly covered iris, eyelashes
    - little of bottom eyelid
4. Mouth - top lip, bottom lip
5. Chin, jawline, cheeks - few, short, lines

### Pen notes

Order

- outline
- hairline
- chin

Spacing

- eyebrows at 1/3
    - eyes as dot dash
- nose at 1/2
    - Nose as line not dots
