## 1. Taoing (ULG)

Heaven and earth<br>
begin in the unnamed;<br>
<strong>name's the mother<br>
of the ten thousand things.</strong>

## 1 (SM)

The unnamable is the eternally real.<br>
Naming is the origin<br>
of all particular things.

## One (JP)

The world began without any names;<br>
The names we use generate for us<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the myriad things.

## 1 (RBB)

Nameless indeed is the source of creation<br>
But things have a mother and she has a name.

## Chapter 1 (AW)

It was from the Nameless that Heaven and Earth spring;<br>
The named is but the mother that rears the ten thousand creatures, each after its kind.

## 1 The Way (BH)

Without-Name is The Origin<br>
Of Heaven and Earth.<br>
Has-Name is the Mother<br>
Of every Earthly Being.

## 1 (JCHW)

As the origin of Heaven-and-Earth, it is nameless:<br>
As "the Mother" of all things, it is nameable.

## 1 Transcending (RAD)

In the infancy of the universe,<br>
there were no names.<br>
Naming fragments the mysteries of life<br>
into then thousand things and their manifestations.

## 14

(The Tao can't be seen, heard, or touched.)

## 14. Celebrating Mystery (ULG)

<strong>Triply undifferentiated,<br>
it merges into oneness</strong>,<br>
not bright above,<br>
not dark below.

## Fourteen

These three complaints amount<br>
To only one, which is<br>
Beyond all resolution.

## Chapter 14 (AW)

These three because they cannot be further scrutinized,<br>
Blend into one.

## 14 (SD & SL)

These three cannot be fully grasped.<br>
Therefore they become one.

## 14 The Way of Heaven (BH)

These three aspects cannot be conveyed<br>
Or examined–<br>
They blend and become The One.

## 14 (JCHW)

These three attributes are unfathomable;<br>
Therefore they fuse into one.

## 14 (DCL)

These three cannot be fathomed<br>
And so they are confused and looked upon as one.

## 14 Beyond Reason (RAD)

Beyond reason, these three merge,<br>
contradicting experience.
