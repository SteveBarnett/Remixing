Notes from [Don't Call It Art by Austin Kleon](https://austinkleon.com/dont-call-it-art/).

## Raw notes

- (Blackout poetry) When the future comes, all experts becomes students

### 1. Throw Out The Instructions

#### Don't Call It Art

- ... **if you don't call it art, you take all the pressure off. Now you can just make stuff.**
- "Were we to meet this figure socially, this accusatory character, this internal critic, this unrelenting fault finder, we would think there is something wrong with [them]."
- Luckily art critics are only interested in art. If you don't call it art, the critics won't care what you're doing, and they'll leave you alone. Now you can do whatever you want.

#### It's Better When You Don't Know

- (Blackout poetry) Not knowing is the first step
- Matrix: Unintended/Deliberate vs Imperfection/Perfection
    - Deliberate Perfection: Boring mastery
    - **Deliberate Imperfection: Punk rock, chance operations, wabi-sabi, Navajo rugs, etc.**
- People who are able to stay creative their whole lives know that the minute they start knowing, it is time to go back to not knowing.

#### Figure It Out For Yourself

- Loop: Mess around 🔁 Find out
- **What we learn on our own goes deeper and sticks with us longer than what we've been taught.**
- When we're forced to learn things on our own, we make our own discoveries.

### Don't Take Things too Seriously

#### Pretend It's A Comedy

- "One of the symptoms of approaching nervous breakdown is the belief that one's work is terribly important." - Bertrand Russell
- "Whatever inspiration is," said the poet Wisława Szymborska, "it's born from a continuous 'I don't know'"
- Anyone who's spent time with little kids knows that **if they ask you "Why?" more than two or three times–which they will!–the world suddenly becomes very strange and unknown to you.**
- "The 'silly question' is the first intimation of some totally novel development." - Alfred North Whitehead
- The Five Whys (lead to) The universe becomes mysterious
