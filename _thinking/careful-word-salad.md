Careful. As in: cautious, painstaking.

As in: attentive, conscientious, deliberate, mindful, precise, prudent, thoughtful, wary.

- Attentive, as in: observant, heedful.
- Conscientious, as in: diligent, fastidious.
- Deliberate, as in: cautious, prudent, purposeful, studious.
- Thoughtful, as in: astute, considerate, tactful.

Studious, as in: contemplative, reflective.
