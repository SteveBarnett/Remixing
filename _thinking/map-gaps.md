Things that aren't in [MAP](/thinking/map/) (yet?).

- proactive, building, preparing, cultivating
- resting in the good
- active nurturing: I dropped the N of RAIN for my LSD!
- anything somatic: good way to hush the critic
- explicitly stating just sitting
- using gathas, four lines

---

kindness through the body, as the somatic equivalent to mirror metta.
something felt, not thought.

1.
touch.
The body answers the gesture before the mind agrees to it.

2.
Easiest through the mirror: give myself what I'd give a friend without thinking twice.

3.
Soften the body around the feeling instead of bracing against it.
Let the out-breath run longer than the in-breath. A few slow ones.

4.
Savour the good feeling. Let it soak in a bit.

---

Using a somatic practice bypass the Critic. Touch doesn't argue.

soft hand, heart, slow.
