## What this is

- **You Know You're Here When...** — The specific crash state this protocol addresses. Read these until you find the one that matches where you are right now.
- **What You've Adapted To** — Why this crash pattern exists. What normalized dysfunction created this specific breakdown.
- **The Protocol** — Three levels of intervention:
    - Immediate Action — What to do in the next 5 minutes
    - Short-Term Stabilization — What to do today
    - Pattern Interrupt — What to do this week
- **The Recovery** — How you'll know you're stabilizing. The signs that the protocol is working.
- **What Comes After** — Where this leads. What becomes possible once you've stopped this particular crash.

### Protocol 4: Stillness Installation - Practice doing nothing

- Compulsive motion that looks like engagement but is **actually avoidance**
- You've normalized perpetual motion. ... **This isn't drive—it's compulsion**. ...  stillness isn't the absence of effort. It's the source of direction.
- Immediate Action. **Do nothing for a tiny block of time. Ask: "What am I avoiding by staying busy?"**
- Short-Term Stabilization. Schedule stillness. Identify your hyperactivity triggers. **Practice stillness between tasks.**
- Pattern Interrupt — **Take one completely unproductive break daily.** End your day with stillness, not collapse. Instead of working until you crash, stop while you still have energy.
- Stop moving long enough to choose your next move.
