## Raw notes from cards

- Decisions are bets on the future
    - **How confident are you on the outcome?**
    - There are many possible outcomes
- **Outcome = Quality of decision + Luck**
    - How much of the outcome was linked to decision quality? To luck? Both have some influence.
- Self-serving bias
    - Good outcome was my skill, bad outcome was my (bad) luck
    - We flip this for other people
- Loss aversion
- Motivated reasoning
    - **We alter out interpretation to match our beliefs**
        - For positive self-image
- **Temporal discounting: present me >>> future me**
- We bet based on our beliefs
- Belief formation:
    1. Hear
    2. Believe
    3. Maybe, sometimes, verify
- **Our brains are wired for preferring false positives to false negatives**
    - Survival!
    - Evolution prefers efficiency to accuracy
- We can't change our brains. We have to work with the limitations we have.
- Like is like Poker, not like Chess
- **We should focus on how unsure we are**
