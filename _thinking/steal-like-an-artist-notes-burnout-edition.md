## Gathered notes

- **Make things to understand yourself**
- Study and steal from many, transform and remix, credit
    - Mine: **Research, remix, replay**
    - Everything is a remix: 1. Copy; 2. Transform; 3. Combine
- Do what you like, **do what you enjoy**
- **Work with your hands**
- **Have several projects on the go, bounce between them**
- Stay curious and kind, be willing to look stupid

## Raw notes

### 1. Steal like an artist

- Nothing is original
- Save your thefts for later

### 2. Don't wait until you know who you are to get started

- Make things, know thyself
- Fake it 'til you make it'
- Start copying
- Good theft: honour, study, steal from many, credit, transform, remix
- Bad theft: degrade, skim, steal from one, plagiarise, imitate, rip off

### 3. Write the book you want to read

- Write what you like (not what you know)

### 4. Use your hands

- Step away from the screen

### 5. Side projects and hobbies are important

- Practice productive procrastination
    - Have a lot of projects going at once so you can bounce between them
    - Take time to get bored
- Don't worry about unity from piece to piece - what unifies all of your work is the fact that you made it

### 6 .The Secret: do goo work and share it with people

- Step 1: wonder at something. Step 2: invite others to wonder with you.
- Share your dots, but don't connect them

### 7. Geography is no longer our master

- Your brain gets too comfortable in your everyday surroundings. You need to make it uncomfortable.

### 8. Be nice (The world is a small town)

- Make friends, ignore enemies
- You will need: curiosity, kindness, stamina, a willingness to look stupid
- Validation is for parking
- Keep a praise file

### 9. Be boring (It's the only way to get work done)

- Take care of yourself

### 10. Creativity is subtraction

- Choose what to leave out
