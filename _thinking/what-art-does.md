## 1. art

- ... **all kinds of things where somebody does more than is absolutely necessary for the sake of the feeling they get by doing it**
- When we speak of an art object we mean 'something that is intended to trigger some feelings.' ... Art is a name we give to a certain type of experience. It's the name for a kind of engagement with something.
- The art engagement begins where the functional engagement ends
- The fact that something is generally seen as an art object does not dictate that we will have an art engagement with it. And the fact that something is generally seen as a functional object does not dictate that we can't have a art engagement with it.
- **Art is a way of making feelings happen**

## 2. feelings

- Feelings are faster (and sometimes completely wrong). Logic and deduction are slower (and also sometimes completely wrong!). Faced with a novel situation that needs a quick reaction, we rely primarily on our fast feelings to navigate.
- Most of the really big choices in our lives aren't based on just the careful application of logic and deduction: we use our feelings.

## 6. where does art start?

- Imagining is the great human skill. It's how we project our minds into possible futures; where we create and test projects that do not presently exist.
- Play is how children learn. Art is how adults play.

## 7. how does art change me?

- "Human beings don't see what is in front of them, they see what is important to them"
- **Art suggests new places to direct our attention**

## 8. how does art change us?

- Art is ... a reservoir of shared experiences that gives us ways of sharing complex feelings with each other. It's the lifeblood, the lubricant, the circulatory system of community. The maintenance of the community.
- **Civilisation is shared imagination.**
- Why does it matter that [a soap opera] is fictional?
    1. we can know about the feelings of all involved (because we 'know' the characters involved)
    2. there are no real-world consequences to our thoughts
- **As a species, we're so phenomenally good at control, that we tend to think it must be the right posture for every problem**

## 9. we start worlds

- ... we are unfinished (and unfinishable) beings whose tasks is to constantly to re-examine and remix our ideas and our identities
- Just as we need science to tell us how the changing world is, we need art to help find out how we feel about it. We need those feelings to guide our decisions and values. **Science discovers, art digests.**
