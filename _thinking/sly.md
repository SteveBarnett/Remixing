The sequel to [LSD](/thinking/lsd/).

- **Slow your tempo**
    - Slow down
    - Pause for just a moment
    - Pay a little more attention
- **Listen**
    - Come as close to the discomfort as is possible
    - The three conversations: what happened, feelings, identity
    - NVC: observation, feelings, needs, request
    - Look for: the centre; the (lack of) edges; the changes
    - Keep don’t-know mind
    - Use [The Constable](/thinking/the-constable/) frame of mind
- **Yield**
    - Transform it using softness, connection, sensitivity, calmness
    - Subtly divert it so that it can't land effectively
