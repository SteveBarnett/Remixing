Comprehend vs Apprehend?

---

Most languages other than English have two types of knowing.

- French savoir / connaître
- German wissen / kennen
- Spanish saber / conocer
- Portuguese saber / conhecer
- Italian sapere / conoscere
- Dutch weten / kennen
- Polish wiedzieć / znać

Mandarin has a three-way split: 知道 zhīdào (know something), 认识 rènshi (know someone), 会 huì (know how to).

## te reo Māori?

- mātau to know, be acquainted with, understand
- [mōhio](https://maoridictionary.co.nz/word/4147) (experience verb) (-hia,-tia) to know, understand, realise, comprehend, recognise. 

## German

- wissen is for knowing information that uses a verb
- kennen is for knowing entities

## French

- Savoir is used when there’s another verb involved. When that verb is an infinitive, the English equivalent is "to know how to."
- Connaître must be used with a direct object, which may be a person, place, or thing. While connaître can be translated by "to know," it may be helpful to think of it as "to be familiar with":

## Wisdom vs Intelligence

- Intelligence is knowing tomato is a fruit.
- Wisdom is knowing not to put a tomato in a fruit salad.

## The line

- Facts
- Embodiment
- Enlightenment, coming to a realisation
- The feel of, the experience of it, not the description
- The description of the thing is not the same same the thing itself
