## Introduction

Work is the thing I have struggled most with over the years. I haven't tended to work long hours, but I have put a lot of my identity, my self-worth, and my life's purpose and meaning in to and on to work. I've had times when I think the problem is me, when I think the problem is the team, when the problem is the organisation, and more besides.

My practice has helped loosen and soften around these things quite a lot. Much of my practice has been Budddist, and Buddhist-adjacent, of various flavours. I'm particularly fond of some flavours of Zen and their focus on living a good enough, ordinary, life. I've also soaked in various flavours of philosophy and psychology, trying to find my way through.

These days "at the office" can mean many things. A few floors of a skyscraper. A hotdesk at a co-working space. A room at home with a standing desk. All these of I consider "at the office" for this book: the place where we do our work.

We're going cover a couple of aspect of work: me, my team, and my organisation. For each one we'll look at how secular dharma intersects with it, and how it can help: identity, conflict, right livelihood. We'll also link to it to Daniel Pink's theory of motivation: autonomy, mastery and purpose.
