Ideas for a [Buddhism in a Nutshell book for Tuwhiri](https://tuwhiri.substack.com/p/generosity-of-heart-and-mind#:~:text=A%20SERIES%20OF%20SHORT%20SECULAR%20DHARMA%20TEXTS).

## Front-runners

Keep away from Buddhist things, or high concepts. Stick to something in the world, something intensely human and human-centred, something concrete and grounded.

- Hobbies
    - Sketching, gardening, video games
    - Links to SB:
        - mindfulness
        - flow states and their transitory-ness
- Work
    - Relationships
    - Conflict
    - Right Livelihood
    - Fertile ground for practice!
    - Links to SB:
        - dependent origination
        - ELSA
        - Care
        - Working with the world
        - Enrichment of the human condition
        - Flourishing for self and others


Angles to feed in:

- Everything is your main practice
- Remixing
- Paradoxes and opposites
- Layers
- Keep Going
- Valued Action and Continued recommitment


## First pass after a big mind-mapping session

- Hobbies
    - Sketching, gardening, video games
    - Everything is your main practice
- Remixing
    - Everything is a Remix
    - Steal like an artist
    - Research, remix, replay
    - Make the teachings your own
    - Flip of upaya, expedient means
- Antifragility
    - Stick to simple rules
    - Respect old habits and rules
    - Avoid known bads instead of looking unknown goods
    - Keep your options open
    - Take lots of small risks
    - Avoid SPOFs
- Paradoxes and opposites
- Layers
    - Pace layers
    - Distinct but not divided
- Work
    - Relationships
    - Conflict
    - Right Livelihood
        - Increase happiness or reduce suffering
    - Everything is your main practice
- Keep Going
    - Persistence
    - "Faith"
- Precepts
- Argumentativeness
- Gates / Fields
    - Everything is your main practice
- Valued Action
    - Continued recommitment
    - Clarity
- Expedient means (not skilful means, sort of)
