- The master's tool will never dismantle the master's house
- There is no simple, monolithic, solution to our big problems, only complex ones
- Revolution poses fundamentally frightening questions to those in power
- When we stay silent, it becomes normalised
- We must enact the world we're aiming for
- We must be willing to cause unhappiness
- Reject or widen the scripts for what counts as good
- We must have a willingness to be blunt
- Take the long view for system change
- Look for root causes, not individuals
- You can't understand a game by looking at the players
- Look at the situation, not the person
- People react angrily when you touch a nerve: it your claim was actually ridiculous it could be easily ignored
- Maintain a critical consciousness
- See actions in relation to paths of least resistance
- Make noise, be seen, break the silence
- Dare to make people uncomfortable
- Your privilege is not a reason for guilt, it is part of your power to be used in support of what you believe
- Do what's right, not what's easy
- Be aware of your advantages, leverage them
- Generosity and vulnerability make your connections open and durable
- Build instead of attack
- Strategies instead of issues
- Engage tension, but don't indulge drama
- Assume best intent, attend to impact
- Acknowledge the complexity, nuance
- Hold the tension
- Be playful, curious, open
- Binary thinking leads to dominance, marginalisation, oppression
- Power is not only what you have, but what the enemy thinks you have
- Make the enemy live up to their own rules
- The price of a successful attack is a constructive alternative
- Act decisively and intuitively
- Keep pressing the advantage
- Every situation is workable
- “Taking whole”. Winning in a way that keeps as much intact as possible for everyone. 
- True victory is victory over aggression. Aggression leads to destruction, brings devastation to all parties.
