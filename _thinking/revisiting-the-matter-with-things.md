## Gathered old notes

### The left hemisphere

- The left hemisphere gets self-righteous, angry, disgusted, lacks nuance
- The left hemisphere is more precise (repeatable, reproducible), less accurate (closeness to truth, to target)

### Some hemisphere poles

| Left | Right |
| --- | --- |
| theory | experience |
| narrow to certainty | open to possibility |
| parts, pieces | whole, union |
| either/or | both/and |
| shallow | deep |
| static | motion |

### Models and Reality

- Our mechanisms for simplifying the world so that we can control is prevent us from understanding it
- Measuring simplifies a complex thing, misses important parts
- Using a model highlights what fits, makes it seem like a good model
- Thinking we're unbiased just hides the bias
- Everything that matters is hard to pin down
- We can only have imprecise knowledge of imprecise things
- Language is poor at capturing subtleties (except metaphor, poetry)
- Who we are determines how we see determines what we find
- The answer you get depends on how you frame the question

### Free speech

- When things can't be questioned, it's a tyranny
- Don't deny people the right to speak
    - Hear them out, then discuss, argue
- Negative Capability: remain in doubt and uncertainty

### The wider picture

- We need both focused attention and broad attention
- There isn't one way of looking at the world, here/now
    - Don't disregard the elsewhere/elsewhen because it doesn't match the present
    - Don't dismiss things because they're not convenient
- You need to be able to argue both sides of an argument

### Thinking as a process

- Thinking is not separate from perceiving, feeling, bodily motion
- Everything exists only in relation to a rich and complex nexus of other things
- Problem (something that can be fixed) vs Predicament (a set of circumstances, not solvable like a problem)
    - Predicaments require a lot of understanding before offering a good response (not a solution)
- Knowledge (can be articulated) vs Understanding (inhabiting a mental space when there's no division between you and the inquiry)
- Falsifying something is hard
    - Instead test against ongoing lived evidence

- [The Slow playbook: offer good, time-tested, alternatives]
