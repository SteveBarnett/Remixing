### Introduction

- I think of **agency** as **the capacity to both see and act on degrees of freedom that other people overlook**.
- It’s being able to **see choices where others only see constraints**
- It’s also the courage to walk through those doors, to **defy** the forces keeping us normal—**the pressures imposed by our inner script and by the boxes other people put us in**.
- Agency entails a radically different way of relating to the world. Rather than avoiding failure and rejection, **agentic people see these as the inevitable costs of personal progress, and opportunities for learning**.
- Rather than taking it for granted that the experts have it under control, they expect that something more can be done, and that they can be the ones to do it.
- **They choose to believe that everything is learnable**, even traits and skills often believed to be innate, like charisma or curiosity.
