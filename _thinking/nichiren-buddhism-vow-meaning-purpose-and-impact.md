- Vow as living engine of Bodhisattva path
- A question that lives inside you, a question you become
- Start asking "what does the world need?"" instead of asking "what do I want?"
- **Obstacles are not punishments, but testing grounds**
    - like honing a sword
- Ask: **how do I use this to fulfil my vow?**
- The vow strengthens our character
