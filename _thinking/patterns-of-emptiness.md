From [Patterns of Emptiness on Waking Up](https://dynamic.wakingup.com/course/CO32DD2?code=SC541514D&pack=PKQEXVB&share_id=5D68FF68&source=content%20share)

- **The mind is a public park**, not a private garden
- We're not trying to get rid of the subject, but see its true status
- Our identity is made of what we accept **and what we reject**
- **Things are revealed in their vanishing**
- The more you think about something, try to make sense of it, the more you solidify it
- **Don't (try to) cover up your mistakes**
- **Community and co-emergence**
