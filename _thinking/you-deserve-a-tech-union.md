#### Step 4: Negotiating Your Contract

- winning your union election will immediately improve things for you and your coworkers. Your employer is required to maintain the status quo at work, and cannot change your working conditions without negotiating with the union

- You’re going to wind down your organizing committee and stand up a new committee—one focused on winning contract negotiations rather than counting union cards. This process will likely be much longer than the organizing phase.
