Grabbing some notes from [Austin Kleon](https://austinkleon.com/)'s Steal Like An Artist, Show Your Work, and Keep Going to try and help with feeling stuck on [WASD](/thinking/work-and-secular-dharma/).

## Gathered notes

### For writing

- Write the book you want to read 
- Establish a daily routine
- Use the end of one to light the next

### For work

- Keep a praise file
- Take people behind the scenes

### For both

- Turn your flow into stock
- Credit is always due

### For practice

- Identify your fellow knuckleballers and sing their praises
- If you're feeling down, make something for someone special
- To exercise is to exorcise

## Raw notes

### Steal Like An Artist

- Write the book you want to read
- Keep a praise file
- Creativity is subtraction
    - Choose what to leave out

### Show Your Work

- Be an amateur
- Take people behind the scenes
- For an intangible product, the process is valuable
- Turn your flow into stock
- Credit is always due
- Identify your fellow knuckleballers
    - Sing their praises
- Chain-smoke: use the end of one to light the next

### Keep Going

- Establish a daily routine
- If you're feeling down, make something for someone special
- When in doubt, tidy up
    - Keep your tools tidy and your materials messy
- To exercise is to exorcise
