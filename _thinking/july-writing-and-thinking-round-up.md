What's the thread that runs thought this month?

- Edges and discomfort
- Path as goal
- Realism, critique, of AI

## Raw notes from [July](/2026/07/)

- Your edges are a koan to live with, not problems to solve
    - When you’re in touch with them, there’s energy and attention and urgency
- Staying in touch with your edges helps you get comfortable with humility and vulnerability
- Deep practice means not having a goal in mind.
- Any desire is an obstacle to practice, even a goal.
- Zazen helps us see how much we like delicious sweets. Zen practice helps us taste the deliciousness of ordinary life.
- The path is the goal
    - A direction, not a destination
    - An orientation, not (attachment to a desired) an outcome
- Every step on the path is the practice, not a way to get further along the path
- An ongoing, never-ending, practice
- Resist with Realism notes

Don't think lightly of battle. Hold on to, keep, your compassion.

- The prize is compassion.
- Thinking lightly of battle, engaging an enemy, makes us lose our compassion.
- Attacking what yields means throwing away compassion.
- Thinking your enemy is evil destroys your compassion, making yourself an enemy.
- The side with compassion prevails.
- The side that takes no delight in battle prevails.
