- **Bright, clear, luminous**
- Happy, playful, wonderful
- Worthy of our deepest attention, full of wonder, precious
- **The sacredness of the everyday**
- Zazen – the sacred act of just being
- **No separation between (apparent) poles**: form and emptiness, sacred and profane, spiritual and material, perfection and imperfection.

## Bright

- The whole universe is one bright pearl. 
- Notice how brightly everything appears.
- Everything appears brightly, luminously, clearly
- Bright like open, luminous, clear.
- The feeling of happy, playful, bright activity
- Everyday things feel sacred, wonderful, and precious

## Everyday

- We shouldn't seek the sacred in a transcendent realm beyond ourselves or our world, but rather let the sacred reveal itself when we quieten, relax our minds, and open our awareness to what it happening right here and now.
- The sacredness of ordinary, everyday, acts.
- Treat the everyday as sacred.
- When everyday things feels sacred, wonderful, and precious.
- Everyday life, filled with noise and the lively activities of people.
- The liturgy of everyday life: of washing the face, using the lavatory, cooking a meal, brushing the teeth.
- It took Dogen to articulate it in many ways and to show that some of the deepest spiritual experiences can be with the particulars of everyday life.
- The koan of everyday life.

## Sacred

- The sacred reveals itself when we're quiet, relaxed, open to what's here and now.
- There’s no separation between form and emptiness, purity and impurity, sacred and profane, spiritual and material, perfection and imperfection. In between any two opposites lies emptiness, creative potential.
- Zazen – The Sacred Act of Just Being
- by sacred, I mean worthy of devotion, full of wonder, inconceivable and ungraspable.
- Sacred: precious, infinite, worthy of our deepest attention.
- When we attempt to separate the sacred from the ordinary, we create a false dichotomy.
