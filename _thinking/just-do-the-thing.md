I'm swirling around the stuff in [Internal Perfectionist Anxiety Habits](http://127.0.0.1:4000/thinking/internal-perfectionist-anxiety-habits/). The other day I was reminded of a turn of phrase that I like:

> Do the thing to bring about the mood; don't wait for the mood to do the thing.

I think this is mostly a note-to-self to make a **tiny** first step.

## Some related things

- Shrink the change. For Rider problems (using [Switch](/notes/heath-bros/switch.jpg) language: that is, when the problem is that you're over-thinking). To get started, keep shrinking the change until the Rider can't say no.
- Remove fear and demotivators rather than increase hope and motivators. From [Tiny Habits](/notes/process/tiny habits.jpg), I think?
- You can't think your way out of a thinking problem.
- Motivation is thinking, tricking yourself with thoughts, angles
    - What I'm looking at/for is more like avoiding (over)thinking, slightly wu wei
- Just do the thing, a bit like [The "just" in "just sitting"](/thinking/zen/the-just-in-just-sitting/)
- [Self-fulfilling activity](/thinking/zen/self-fulfilling-activity/)
- Insight comes from action, not vice versa
- Priority, weighting
- Setting a default option (is this just habit by another name?)
- Find a feeling to get the elephant moving
    - Destination postcard
- Find a bright spot
- Target the Constraint
- Start with a Burst
    - Minimise the middle by looking backwards near the start and looking forwards near the end
- Go where the energy is
- Identify failure quickly
