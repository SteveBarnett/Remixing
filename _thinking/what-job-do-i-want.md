1. What have I liked about previous jobs?
2. What have I not liked about previous jobs?
3. What am I better at?
4. What am I worse at?
5. Look at previous steps: what do I like and I'm good at.
6. Look at previous steps: what do I not like and I'm bad at.
7. For the previous two steps, what's the underlying category or type or idea? Use these as a filter when looking at new jobs.
