A reminder from [my mirror metta](/thinking/my-mirror-metta/):

- I find it easy to direct metta outwards, I find it very difficult to direct metta inwards.
- I find it powerful when I imagine someone directing metta at me, especially when it's value-aligned.
- The "trick" is to mirror it.

---

I tend to have a problem with bravery. Especially when tired or stressed or both.

Ways to hack it:

- just get started, using a silly trick
- the Zen approach of no preferences
- use a cousin of mirror metta, which is what I want to work out here.

Current state:

- I find it fairly easy to be brave for other people
- I appreciate and admire other people being brave
- I tend to spiral quickly into cowardice, when I fall into it

Mirror metta:

- be clear on what I'm struggling with;
- direct metta at someone who's struggling with something similar;
- imagine them reflecting, directing that same metta back at me.

A reminder from You Can Just Do Things: silly tricks are fine, if they work!

Mirror mana (name?): "imaginary friend."

- Imagine someone else is there and wants your help
- Imagine someone is asking for your help
- Frame it as being brave for someone else
